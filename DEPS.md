# Dependencies

Policy (ADR-7): `orbit-gc`, `orbit-runtime` (formerly `orbit-core`, `orbit-vm` and `orbit-stdlib`) and `orbit-compiler` take no runtime dependencies. Any other runtime dependency is added here in the same PR that introduces it, with its reason and the measured change in release binary size. Dev-dependencies are listed too but are held to a lighter bar, since they don't ship.

Candidates under evaluation:

- `clap` or `lexopt`: argument parsing for `orbit-cli`.

Rejected: `gc-arena` and `boa_gc` (ADR-2 chose our own collector, `orbit-gc`). They remain only in the throwaway `spike/` workspace.
